Unreal Nova 2000 — prototype 03

An independent visual prototype inspired by late-1990s 3D games. Not affiliated with Epic Games.

The coastal sky cubemap, moss, wood, rust, sand, floor, bark and concrete textures, and the fern and heather cutouts were reused from the user-provided Rannamets 0.2 project.
Wind, canopy, surf, footsteps and birds use the original v9 Rannamets Sound synthesis and mix. Version 11 retains the approved menu song unchanged. Landmark soundscapes use processed excerpts of the user-supplied Desulfosarcina, Monteyoung_v2 and Doom recordings, shortwave recordings and quiet vinyl texture. The number-station voice is generic synthetic speech produced with FFmpeg/libflite, with fictional sequences. New tape melodies refer to the mood of Boards of Canada without sampling or transcribing that piece. Exact sources and edits are documented in assets/audio/README.md and assets/audio/manifest.json.
Scots pine and birch branch textures were generated for this prototype.
Version 73 tree bark: seven species-specific photographs and corrected materials imported from Unreal Nova 2000 Waldlabor revision 3. Authors, source photographs, crop details, CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 licenses and adaptation notices are retained in BARK_CREDITS.txt, bark-sources.json and the game menu.
Version 72 rowan fruit skins: George Chernilevsky, "Sorbus aucuparia 2022 G1", CC BY-SA 4.0. The unchanged Wikimedia photograph is applied to individual 3D berries using circular UV regions, lighting and slight color variation. Source, download and license: ROWAN_CREDITS.md; https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sorbus_aucuparia_2022_G1.jpg ; https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
The complete 96-species catalog, habitat descriptions and plant texture cutouts are reused from Rannamets 0.2. Species-specific reference credits and source links are retained in flora-sources.json. New growth-form geometry and clustered placement are implemented in flora.js and terrain.js. Species shapes are illustrative; the game is not a validated identification key.
